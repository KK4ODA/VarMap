Position TX: broadcasting my position through VarAC

      
This sends a VarAC broadcast message containing your coordinates. It is separate from VarAC's own beacon, which carries only your grid square on VarAC's timer.

Smart timing

      
Smart timing is not allowed on VarAC's calling frequencies (14.105, 7.105, 3.595 and the rest of VarAC's standard list): it is meant for a tracker frequency you have QSY'd to. On a calling frequency use Fixed interval. VarMap checks VarAC's current frequency and blocks smart timing there automatically.

      

        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
      

      
You, the licensee, are responsible for every transmission this software initiates. Position TX is OFF by default and starts in dry-run mode. A broadcast discloses your exact coordinates on HF; reduce the decimals for a coarser position. Built-in limits that no setting can override: at least 5 minutes between transmissions, a stationary station repeats itself at most every 30 minutes, at most 6 transmissions per hour (default 2) and 48 per day, and no smart timing on VarAC's calling frequencies.

      
Diagnostics

      

        
        Opens VarAC's broadcast window, fills it, checks VarAC's byte counter, then closes it without sending. Run after a VarAC update.
